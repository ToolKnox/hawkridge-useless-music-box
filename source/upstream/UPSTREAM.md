<p><strong>Introduction</strong><br><br>This is my version of the Useless Box. I added a buzzer and now it can play tunes. I printed everthing with a 0.6 nozzle and used supports, but I think you can do without supports.<br><br>The box uses two mg90s servos. You could use the sg90s version as well. Printing all the parts took about 5 hours.<br><br>You can find the code for the Arduino Nano (or clone) here:&nbsp;<br><br><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" class="editor-link" href="https://github.com/Dawied/Useless-Music-Box">https://github.com/Dawied/Useless-Music-Box</a></p><p>The code uses music from Robson Cauto's project "Songs for Arduino", thanks!<br><br><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" class="editor-link" href="https://dragaosemchama.com/en/2019/02/songs-for-arduino/">https://dragaosemchama.com/en/2019/02/songs-for-arduino/</a><br><br><br><strong>Bill of Materials</strong></p><ul><li><p>1 Arduino Nano or equivalent</p></li><li><p>1 Nano expansion board</p></li><li><p>1 18650 battery</p></li><li><p>1 18650 battery holder</p></li><li><p>1 flip on/off swicth (for main power)</p></li><li><p>1 toggle on/off switch (for operation)</p></li><li><p>2x MG90s or SG90s 180° servos</p></li><li><p>1 Passive buzzer</p></li><li><p>8 M2*4 screws</p></li><li><p>Dupont wires (for buzzer and battery holder)<br></p></li></ul><figure class="image image-style-align-center"><img class="editor-image-resize" src="https://media.printables.com/media/prints/1747960/rich_content/991b04ad-40d5-4db2-abcc-fcf84ee3ee36/electronic-parts-annotated-small.jpg#%7B%22uuid%22%3A%2273182b9f-6147-44e6-9450-019d0129e247%22%2C%22w%22%3A1024%2C%22h%22%3A1059%7D"></figure><p><br><strong>Assembling</strong><br><br>Use male dupont wires to solder on the 5V output of the 18650 battery holder. Place the main switch between the + of the battery holder and one of the V pins of the expansion board<br><br>Use the M2*4 screws to mount the expansion board and battery holder in the box<br><br>Fit male dupon wires on the buzzer and click the buzzer in the buzzer holder of the box<br><br>Fit the toggle switch into the hole of the lid and secure with the screws.<br><br>To correctly fit the fingers on the servos, connect the servos to the expansion board and run the code one time, this will put the servos in the resting position.<br><br>Now fix the switch finger making sure it stop a few millimeters before the back of the box. Fit the lid finger making sure it rests a few millimeters above the battery.<br><br>Fit the MG90 servos into the retainers<br><br>You can use a piece of filament for the hinge.
